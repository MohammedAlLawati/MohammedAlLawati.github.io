About

I'm a renewable energy engineer with 3+ years designing and delivering grid-tied solar PV systems at an Omani EPC company. I was the design and stakeholder-approval focal point on PDO's Project NOOR, which installed 2+ MW of residential rooftop PV across the Sultanate.

My work spans PVsyst and HelioScope simulation, techno-economic feasibility and LCOE analysis, commissioning, cost estimation and Python automation. I'm currently completing an MSc in Hydrogen Technology and Economy as a Shell Nawafidh Scholar, and I'm interested in solar PV, battery energy storage (BESS) and power systems engineering roles across the GCC.
